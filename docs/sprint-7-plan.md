## 0. Where I depart from the design, and why

| Design shows | This plan does | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Available to withdraw" and payouts | A real per-store **ledger**; payouts are **created and completed by staff**; no seller "Withdraw" | Money cannot move without a payment provider (Q9). A button that does nothing is worse than none. The ledger is the part that is hard to retrofit, so it is built now. |
| Seller "Update Status" on a transaction (Q65) | Staff-only | A seller must not mark their own payout paid. |
| Edit popup: 6 on/off toggles; details page: 8 sections × View / View + Manage (Q67) | One model: a level per section — `none`, `view`, `manage` — for all eight; roles are presets | The details page is the richer truth. A toggle is `manage ⇔ on`. |
| An activity log of what a member did (Q70) | Explicit, human-worded entries from the actions that matter | Logging every request would produce "PATCH /seller/products/…", which is exactly what the design avoids. |
| A commission that makes R1,240 → R1,120 (Q64) | `Country.commission_rate_bps`, edited in admin, seeded at **500 (5 %)** | 5 % is what the design's numbers imply. It is per market because fees are a market decision. |

## 1. Team / Managers (`team` app) — built first

Why first: every later router must be permission-aware, and "who sees which notification" depends on it.

**Models**
- `StoreMember`: `store`, `user` (null until the invite is accepted), `email`, `role`, `permissions` JSON
  `{module: none|view|manage}`, `status` (`invited` | `active` | `suspended`), `invited_by`,
  `invite_token_hash` (sha256 — the raw token exists only in the e-mail), `invite_expires_at` (7 days),
  `joined_at`, `last_active_at`. Unique `(store, email)`.
- The **owner is implicit** (`Store.owner`): always every permission, never a row, cannot be removed. The list
  endpoint synthesises the Owner row so the table matches the design.
- `StoreActivity`: `store`, `actor`, `action` (slug), `summary` ("Updated price for Wireless Earbuds Pro"),
  `target_type` / `target_id`, `created_at`. Append-only.

**Sections** — the design's eight: `dashboard, orders, products, inventory, customers, marketing, analytics,
transactions`. Mapping of what already exists: chat inbox → `customers`; delivery options → `products`;
header search → `dashboard`. **Owner only:** team management and the plan page (the design has no section for
them). `marketing` exists in the model but guards nothing yet (Q69).

**Roles (presets)** — Manager, Inventory Manager, Order Manager, Marketing Manager, Finance Manager, Support
Agent, Custom. A role sets default levels; the owner may override any section afterwards (the popup), and the
role reads `custom` once the levels stop matching the preset.

**Enforcement** — `StoreAuth(section="orders")` on each router. `GET`/`HEAD` need `view`, everything else
`manage`; otherwise `403 permission_denied` naming the section. Premium gating stays a separate, store-level
check. The store is the one the user **owns**, else the one they are an **active member** of; `X-Store: <id>`
chooses when there are several. `request.member` is `None` for the owner. `last_active_at` is touched at most
once a minute. `StoreAuth` stops inheriting `SellerAuth`: the owner path still requires `is_seller`, a member
does not need it (they never filled in an application). A removed or suspended member loses access on their
next request, though their token is still valid.

**Endpoints** — `/seller/team` (owner only): list with the four KPI cards and filters (status, role, last
active, `q`); `POST /invites {email, role, permissions?}`; `POST /{id}/resend`; `GET|PATCH|DELETE /{id}`;
`GET /{id}/activity`; `GET /roles` (presets with their levels, so the UI never hard-codes them).
`POST /auth/team/accept {token, full_name?, password?}` — an existing account must be signed in as the invited
e-mail; otherwise one is created (e-mail marked verified, because the link arrived there).
`GET /seller/me` → store, `is_owner`, effective permissions: the sidebar hides what you cannot open.
Invite links use a new `PORTAL_URL` setting (falls back to `STOREFRONT_URL`).

**Activity logged at first** (called from the service layer): product price / stock / status changes, order status
changes, CSV exports, delivery-option edits, team changes.

## 2. Transactions (`ledger` app)

**Models**
- `LedgerEntry` (append-only): `store`, `type` (`sale` | `fee` | `refund` | `adjustment` | `payout`), signed
  `amount_cents`, `currency`, `status` (`pending` | `processing` | `completed` | `failed`), `order`, `payout`,
  `reference` ("TXN-10482", from a locked counter row), `description`, `available_at`, `created_by`.
  Unique `(order, store, type)` for `sale`, `fee` and `refund` — the idempotency guarantee.
- `Payout`: `store`, `amount_cents`, `status` (`processing` | `completed` | `failed`), `reference`,
  `created_by`, `completed_at`, `note`.
- `Country.commission_rate_bps` (migration; 500 for the seeded markets).

**Posting rules**
- **Payment confirmed** (the one event is `orders.services.on_payment_confirmed`, called where `set_status`
  moves an unpaid order to paid, and later by the PSP webhook) → per store: `sale` = its line totals − its voucher
  share (`store_discount_cents`) + its `OrderDelivery.price_cents`; `fee` = −commission on (lines − discount).
  `available_at = now + the order's return window`: money is held until a return can no longer come.
- **A store cancels or returns its lines, or staff cancel/refund the order** → `refund` = −what that store's
  `sale` posted, and the `fee` is reversed (+). Once per store per order.
- **Staff adjustment** → `adjustment`, signed (Q66), note required.
- **Payout** → staff create it for ≤ available balance → a `payout` entry (−amount, `processing`); completing
  it marks both `completed` and notifies the seller; failing it reverses the entry.

**Balances are computed, never stored.** Available = Σ entries with `available_at ≤ now` (payouts count at
once); Pending payout = Σ processing payouts; Total sales = Σ `sale` in the period; Total fees = −Σ `fee`. A
sale/fee row reads `completed` once its `available_at` has passed.

**Endpoints** — `/seller/transactions/overview?period=` (the four cards, recent 5, the summary: Sales Revenue,
Platform Fees, Refunds, Adjustments, Net Payout); `/seller/transactions?tab=&q=&status=&date_from=&date_to=&amount_min=&amount_max=`
with tab counts; `/{reference}` (order, customer, payment method, amount breakdown); `/export.csv`. Staff:
`POST /admin/ledger/adjustments`, `/admin/payouts` (create, list, `PATCH /{id}/status`). Section: `transactions`.

`manage.py backfill_ledger` posts entries for paid orders that already exist (idempotent), so existing stores
open to real numbers. Sales Analytics' refunds and adjustments stop being a constant 0 and read from the ledger
(Q50), and its definition of net is documented once.

## 3. Notifications (`notifications` app)

- `StoreNotification`: `store`, `category` (`orders` | `payments` | `inventory` | `customers` | `system`),
  `kind`, `title`, `body`, `link_type` / `link_id`, `created_at`.
- `NotificationRead`: unique `(notification, user)` — read state is **per person**, now that several people share
  a store.
- A member sees a category only if they can `view` its section (orders → orders, payments → transactions,
  inventory → inventory, customers → customers); `system` is for everyone.

| Event | Emitted from |
|---|---|
| New order received + Payment received | `on_payment_confirmed` (the same hook as the ledger), once per store in the order |
| Order cancelled | buyer cancels, or staff cancel |
| Low stock / Out of stock | `refresh_stock_status` **when the status changes** into low/out — once per crossing, not per sale |
| New customer | first paid order from that customer at this store (same key as `portal.customers`) |
| Payout processed | staff complete a payout |
| System | product approved / rejected, plan changed |

Emitted inside the same transaction as the event, so a rollback takes the notification with it.
Endpoints: `GET /seller/notifications?tab=&page=` (the client groups Today / Yesterday by `created_at`),
`GET /summary` (unread per tab and in total, for the header badge), `POST /{id}/read`, `POST /read-all`.
In-app only (e-mail and push need the worker, Q48).

## 4. Product Analytics

`/seller/analytics/products` gains `daily: [{date, orders, units, revenue_cents, views}]` for the Product
Performance chart (its four tabs) and `low_performers`: lowest conversion among products with at least 20 views
in the period (Q72). `slow_movers` stays.

## 6. Assumptions I need a yes or no on

1. **Commission 5 %**, per country, editable in admin.
2. **Payouts held until the return window closes**; only staff create and complete them.
3. **Team management is owner-only**, and seats are free and unlimited (not a premium feature).
4. **`PORTAL_URL`** is a new setting for invite links — what is the seller portal's address?
5. **Marketing** is a permission with nothing behind it yet.
6. **Notifications are in-app only** for now.
7. **Low performers** = lowest conversion among products with ≥ 20 views.
8. Adjustments are **staff-created and signed**.
